# Privacy Policy for DartzVibe

**Last updated: 2026-03-24**

## Overview

DartzVibe is a darts scoring app that runs entirely on your device. Your privacy is important, and the simplest way to protect it is to not collect any data at all.

## Data Collection

DartzVibe does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

- **No analytics or tracking** -- No analytics services are used.
- **No advertising** -- There are no ads or ad-related SDKs.
- **No account required** -- You do not need to sign up or log in.

## Data Storage

All data (player profiles, game history, scores, and settings) is stored **locally on your device only**. This data is never transmitted anywhere. If you uninstall the app, all data is deleted.

## Third-Party Services

DartzVibe does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

## Children's Privacy

DartzVibe does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If data practices change in the future, this page will be updated accordingly.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/OsaSoft/dartzvibe).
